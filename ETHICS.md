# Ethics and responsible use

Visual experience is intimate data. OpenVision treats privacy, consent, provenance, and human agency as system requirements rather than optional product features.

## Principles

- **Consent:** obtain meaningful permission from the recorder and affected people where required.
- **Data minimization:** collect only the modalities needed for the stated purpose.
- **Local control:** prefer local processing, encryption, explicit sharing, and revocable access.
- **Bystander protection:** support redaction and avoid normalizing covert recording.
- **Provenance:** distinguish captured, reconstructed, inferred, and generated material.
- **Honest limitations:** do not present a reconstruction as a person's exact memory or subjective experience.
- **Accessibility:** consider differences in vision and avoid treating one observer or display as universal.

## Prohibited project direction

The project does not support using OpenVision to facilitate unlawful surveillance, non-consensual intimate recording, identity abuse, deceptive evidence fabrication, or human-rights violations.

Responsible-use controls and data rights will evolve alongside the technical specification.
