# Roadmap

This roadmap is directional and will evolve through public proposals.

## Phase 0: Foundation

- Define vocabulary, boundaries, ethics, and governance.
- Publish the OVX 0.1 draft.
- Establish representative, legally shareable test recordings.
- Define basic temporal and geometric validation.

## Phase 1: Replayable

- Capture first-person video, IMU pose, and camera calibration on a consumer device.
- Synchronize streams against a documented time base.
- Package the result as an OVX recording.
- Replay orientation and field of view in a browser.

## Phase 2: Enterable

- Add stereo and depth inputs.
- Support limited positional movement and WebXR playback.
- Measure visual comfort and spatial consistency.

## Phase 3: Reconstructable

- Integrate SLAM and scene-reconstruction pipelines.
- Enable six-degree-of-freedom viewing for suitable scenes.
- Make captured and synthesized regions visibly inspectable.

## Phase 4: Perceptually consistent

- Explore HDR and color appearance reconstruction.
- Add gaze-aware coding and rendering.
- Adapt playback to display characteristics and individual visual needs.
- Develop perceptual evaluation protocols.
