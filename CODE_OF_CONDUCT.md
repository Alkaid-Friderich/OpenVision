# Code of conduct

OpenVision is committed to a respectful, inclusive, and technically rigorous community.

Participants are expected to:

- engage with ideas and evidence rather than personal attacks;
- welcome different backgrounds, abilities, and levels of experience;
- respect privacy, consent, and cultural differences around recording;
- give actionable, good-faith feedback;
- disclose relevant conflicts of interest.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, and deliberate privacy violations are not acceptable. Maintainers may edit or remove content and restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

Concerns may be reported privately to a maintainer through their GitHub profile until a dedicated reporting channel is established.
